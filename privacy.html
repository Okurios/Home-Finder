Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Applies to HomeFinder Portal

1. Who We Are

HomeFinder is a university project property portal that allows users to browse, save, and inquire about residential and commercial property listings. As a data controller we are responsible for the personal data you provide when using this service.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

  • Contract performance — processing your registration and inquiries to provide the service you requested.
  • Legitimate interests — maintaining anonymised audit logs for security purposes.
  • Consent — optional marketing emails (if you ticked the marketing checkbox at registration).

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To send one-time password (OTP) verification codes via email.
  • To forward property inquiries to the relevant agent and deliver their replies to you.
  • To confirm or update viewing appointments.
  • To restore your saved/favourite property list across sessions.
  • To detect and prevent unauthorised account access (audit logs).

5. Data Retention

  • Account data — retained until you delete your account.
  • Session tokens — expire automatically after 24 hours.
  • OTP codes — expire after 10 minutes and are deleted on use.
  • Audit logs — retained for 12 months for security purposes; your identity is removed if you delete your account.

7. Security Measures

  • Passwords stored as bcrypt hashes (cost factor 12) — never readable by anyone.
  • All data in transit encrypted via HTTPS (TLS 1.2+).
  • Two-factor authentication via email OTP on every login.
  • Single-session enforcement — signing in on a new device invalidates previous sessions.
  • Rate limiting on login and OTP endpoints to prevent brute-force attacks.
  • IP addresses anonymised before storage (last octet masked).

8. Cookies

We use a single httpOnly session cookie (hf_token) to keep you logged in for 24 hours. This cookie:

  • Contains no personal data — only an opaque session identifier.
  • Is not accessible to JavaScript (httpOnly flag).
  • Is transmitted only over HTTPS in production (Secure flag).
  • Is deleted automatically when it expires or when you sign out.

We use no tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies of any kind.

9. Third-Party Services

  • Turso (database) — cloud SQLite host. Data stored in EU region.
  • Render.com (hosting) — cloud hosting platform. EU data processing agreement in place.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — used to geocode property addresses (property data only, no user data sent).
  • Google Fonts — font delivery via CDN (no personal data shared).
  • Unsplash — stock photos for demo property images.

10. Contact & Complaints

For any privacy-related queries or to exercise your rights, please contact the data controller via the university project submission channel. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority (in the UK: the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk).

Note: HomeFinder is a university coursework project. While we have implemented GDPR-compliant practices as part of the software design requirements, this is not a commercial service and the data collected is for demonstration purposes only.
